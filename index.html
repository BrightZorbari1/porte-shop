03 · The craft

Made slowly.
Carried fast.

Every Porté piece starts as a paper sketch pinned to the atelier wall, and ends months later — after seventeen prototypes, a dissertation-level debate about zippers, and one final test we call “the overloaded sprint to the train”.

If it survives that, it earns the mark. If it doesn't, we start again. That's why we can promise repairs for life: we already know exactly how every seam was sewn.
