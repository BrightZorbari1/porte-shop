Our story — Est. 2019

We make things for
the way you move.

Porté began with a simple irritation: everything we carried — bags, lunch gear, shoes, suitcases — was either beautiful and fragile, or tough and joyless. We thought the aisle between “fashion” and “function” was a failure of imagination. So we built an atelier in the middle of it.

“The suitcase that survives baggage claim, the lunchbox that survives the commuter crush, the tote that survives your life — that is our entire business plan.”

Inês Duarte — Founder & Head of Making

Today we are seventeen people in a sun-flooded atelier in Porto, making the four things humans never leave home without: something to carry, something to eat from, something to walk in, and something to run for the gate with.

Every material is chosen for how it ages, not how it photographs. Canvas that softens like a favourite jacket. Leather that keeps receipts of every trip in its patina. Soles that re-grip instead of wearing out — because the most sustainable product is the one you never replace.

And when life eventually wins — a strap, a sole, a zipper — our repair bench takes it from there. Free. Forever. That is not a warranty; it is a relationship.

Craft over churn

Seventeen prototypes per piece. A dissertation-level debate per zipper. We release four collections a decade's worth of thought, not four a year.

Circular by default

98% recycled canvas, vegetable-tanned offcut leather, and a repair bench that keeps every Porté in circulation — and out of landfill — for life.

Carriers, not customers

A 40,000-strong club that votes on colours, road-tests prototypes on actual commutes, and writes us postcards we genuinely frame.

The road so far

2019

One sewing machine, a garage in Lisboa, and a tote that outlived its maker's patience for bad bags.

2021

The first Bento Stack ships — lunch stops being an afterthought and becomes an event.

2023

The repair for life promise launches. 40,000 carriers across 38 countries.

2026

Atelier Nº7 opens in Porto. Seventeen people, four crafts, one obsession.
